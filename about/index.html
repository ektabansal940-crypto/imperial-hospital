Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our hospital services, facilities, and patient care support.

Why should I choose Imperial Hospital And Trauma Centre?

Patients can trust Imperial Hospital And Trauma Centre for ethical medical practices, disciplined care, advanced technology, and experienced doctors focused on patient safety and comfort.

Do you provide 24x7 medical services?

Yes, our hospital offers 24×7 emergency and casualty services with continuous ICU support and on-call specialists to manage critical situations effectively.

What facilities are available for patients and relatives?

We provide modern patient rooms, ICU waiting lounges, in-house pharmacy, canteen services, parking facilities, and dedicated support areas for patient attendants.

Are cashless insurance facilities available?

Yes, we are empaneled with major insurance companies and TPAs to provide hassle-free cashless mediclaim services for eligible patients.

How can I book an appointment or contact the hospital?

Patients can contact our hospital through phone (+91 86557 75000) or email to schedule appointments, enquire about services, or receive assistance from our administrative team.
