About Me

Dr. Vaibhav J Bhastana

Dr. Bhastana completed his MBBS at Seth GS Medical College & KEM Hospital, Mumbai, from 2010 to 2016, where he was trained in all fundamental aspects of medical sciences and patient care.

Dr. Vaibhav Jayesh Bhastana is a dedicated Interventional & Structural Cardiologist with over 10+ years of experience in the field of cardiology. He specializes in complex coronary angioplasties and structural cardiological interventions, ensuring that patients receive comprehensive and compassionate care.

Specialized Care

Areas of Expertise

Comprehensive interventional and structural cardiac care addressing TAVI, MitraClip, and evidence-based treatment strategies for every patient.

  • TAVI (Transcatheter Aortic Valve Implantation)

    Minimally invasive valve replacement procedure for patients with severe aortic stenosis.

  • MitraClip Procedures

    Advanced catheter-based repair for mitral valve regurgitation without open-heart surgery.

  • Complex Coronary Angioplasty

    Specialized treatment for severe or multi-vessel coronary artery blockages.

  • Structural Heart Interventions

    Precision procedures to correct structural abnormalities of the heart.

  • Coronary Artery Disease Management

    Comprehensive care to diagnose, treat, and prevent progression of coronary disease.

  • Elective & Emergency Internal Medicine Cases

    Skilled management of both planned and urgent medical conditions.
