What Is a Cathlab?

A Cardiac Catheterisation Laboratory, commonly known as a Cathlab, is a specialised hospital unit where minimally invasive procedures are performed to diagnose and treat cardiovascular diseases. Unlike traditional open-heart surgery, many procedures carried out in a cathlab require only a small incision, reducing pain, hospital stay, and recovery time.

An advanced Cathlab Facilities In Malad is equipped with high-resolution imaging systems that allow cardiologists to visualise blood vessels, heart chambers, and coronary arteries in real time. A reputed Cardiac Catheterisation Lab In Malad provides accurate diagnosis while offering advanced interventional treatments that improve patient safety and clinical outcomes.

Common Procedures Performed in a Cathlab

A well-equipped Cathlab Facilities In Malad performs a wide range of diagnostic and therapeutic procedures for patients with heart and vascular diseases.

Coronary Angiography

Coronary angiography is one of the most common procedures performed in a cathlab. It helps doctors identify blockages or narrowing in the coronary arteries using a special contrast dye and X-ray imaging. An experienced Cardiac Catheterisation Lab In Malad provides accurate imaging to support effective treatment planning.

Coronary Angioplasty

Angioplasty is a minimally invasive procedure used to open blocked coronary arteries and restore healthy blood flow to the heart. A modern Cathlab Facilities In Malad uses balloons and stents to treat arterial blockages, reducing the risk of heart attacks and improving cardiac function.

Pacemaker Implantation

Patients with abnormal heart rhythms may require a pacemaker to regulate their heartbeat. A specialised Cardiac Catheterisation Lab In Malad performs pacemaker implantation safely using advanced imaging guidance.

Balloon Valvuloplasty

This procedure is used to treat narrowed heart valves by widening the affected valve using a balloon catheter. A trusted Cathlab Facilities In Malad offers this minimally invasive treatment for suitable patients, improving blood flow and heart function.

Peripheral Angiography

Peripheral angiography helps diagnose blockages in blood vessels supplying the arms, legs, kidneys, and other organs. A reputed Cardiac Catheterisation Lab In Malad provides detailed vascular imaging to guide further treatment.

Peripheral Angioplasty

Patients with narrowed peripheral arteries may benefit from angioplasty performed in a modern Cathlab Facilities In Malad. This procedure improves blood circulation and reduces symptoms such as leg pain during walking.
